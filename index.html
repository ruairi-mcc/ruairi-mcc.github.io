About Me

I’m a scientist at heart with a love for solving real-world problems using data. With a BSc in Chemistry and an MSc in Data Science, I’ve spent over 12 years working across R&D, manufacturing, and the medical device world — always at the intersection of quality, technology, and practical problem-solving. My career has let me dive deep into everything from process design and quality engineering in GMP environments to building data-driven tools that improve decision-making and drive efficiency. I’ve worked with systems like LIMS and MES, automated reporting processes, and applied machine learning and statistical modelling to tackle complex challenges — all with the goal of making operations smarter and more reliable. I enjoy turning messy, complicated data into clear, actionable insights. Whether it’s optimizing quality systems, improving manufacturing processes, or exploring new ways to apply AI and analytics, I bring curiosity, creativity, and a hands-on approach to every project. Outside of work, I’m always looking for new things to learn — whether that’s diving into the latest in data science or experimenting with new ideas. I believe good communication and teamwork are just as important as technical skills, and I enjoy collaborating with people to make great ideas happen.
